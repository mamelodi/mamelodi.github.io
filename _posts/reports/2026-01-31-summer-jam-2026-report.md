Summer Jam 2026 ran from 3 to 10 January at the University of Pretoria's Mamelodi Vista Campus. Learners spent mornings on Mathematics and English and afternoons in workshops ranging from chess and debate to sport, drama, and choir. This was the first Summer Jam to formally include primary school learners, and demand once again exceeded the places available.

Feedback from volunteers and parents was strongly positive, and catering improved noticeably on past years. The programme was delivered for less than in 2025, but with very little new income it was funded almost entirely from reserves, underlining the need for new donor support.

## Key figures

- **Learners:** 354 registered from 62 secondary and 19 primary schools, plus 108 on the waiting list
- **Volunteers:** 63, including 14 from Cru (USA)
- **Volunteer feedback:** 61% of respondents were returning volunteers; 88% rated their experience 4 or 5 out of 5; 94% would volunteer again
- **Operating cost:** R152,090, down from R191,092 in 2025
