Winter Jam 2026 was a three-week academic programme during the June–July school holidays, focused on Mathematics and English for learners in Grades 6 to 12. MI brought back an earlier approach of using pre-test results to group learners by ability rather than by grade alone, then re-tested at the end of the programme. Mathematics scores rose significantly in Grades 6, 10, and 11, with especially strong gains in division and fractions. Essay-writing scores rose significantly in Grades 6 and 9.

Operationally, catering ran more smoothly and at lower cost than in past years. The main lessons for future programmes are to recruit additional volunteers to cover mid-programme departures, make volunteer training more practical, and strengthen communication and engagement with parents.

## Key figures

- **Reach:** 380 learners from 80 schools; 55 volunteers
- **Grade 6 mathematics:** 5.2 → 9.8 out of 40; **Grade 10 mathematics:** 6.5 → 10.7 out of 40
- **Grade 6 essays:** 2.6 → 5.1 out of 10; **Grade 9 essays:** 4.0 → 6.2 out of 10
- **Volunteers:** 88% rated their experience 4 or 5 out of 5; 97% would return
- **Cost:** R275,176 against a budget of R358,650 (23% under)
- **BackaBuddy campaign:** R12,303 raised
