In 2025, The Mamelodi Initiative delivered its three core programmes (Summer Jam, Road to Finals, and Winter Jam), reaching learners from more than 60 schools. Assessment results showed significant gains in several grades, and the organisation strengthened its team with a new Operations and Programmes Intern, staff development (training and counselling) through Africa Unite, and in-kind support from Rock Risk Insurance.

The year also brought financial challenges. Donation income declined by about 40% compared with 2024, and the year closed with a deficit that placed pressure on MI's reserves. In response, the organisation is prioritising long-term financial sustainability: building dedicated fundraising capacity, growing its base of monthly and local donors, developing corporate and grant partnerships, and aligning programme plans with secured income.

## Highlights

- **Summer Jam 2025:** 284 learners from 56 schools; significant mathematics gains in Grades 11 and 12
- **Winter Jam 2025:** 374 learners from 63 schools, averaging 291 per day over 16 days
- **Road to Finals:** 115–140 learners attending Saturday classes at Tsako Thabo Secondary School in the first quarter
- **Donation income:** R757,650, down from R1,258,262 in 2024
- **Total expenditure:** R1,020,517, down from R1,214,436
- **Result for the year:** deficit of R262,085 (audit pending)
